# ClinicFlow — Beta Onboarding Playbook (TASK-048, DOC4 §12)

## Design Partner Program

### Target: 5-10 independent PT clinics for beta

### Selection Criteria
- 1-10 therapists per clinic
- US-based (HIPAA compliance)
- Currently using disconnected tools (scheduling + billing + paper notes)
- Willing to provide candid feedback
- Representativeness of target market (mix of single/multi-location)

### Beta Timeline (8 weeks)

| Week | Activity | Deliverable |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | Kickoff call + data import | Tenant provisioned, users created |
| 2 | Training session (1hr) | Staff trained on scheduling + patients |
| 3 | Go-live with scheduling | First appointments booked in ClinicFlow |
| 4 | Start clinical documentation | First SOAP notes written |
| 5 | Enable billing + claims | First claims generated |
| 6 | Patient portal rollout | First patients using self-service |
| 7 | Feedback collection | Structured feedback survey + 1hr call |
| 8 | Retrospective + roadmap | Beta completion report |

### Success Criteria
- [ ] 90% of appointments booked through ClinicFlow (not phone)
- [ ] 80% of SOAP notes completed within 24 hours of appointment
- [ ] At least 10 claims submitted through the system
- [ ] At least 5 patients use the portal for booking
- [ ] NPS score ≥ 7 from clinic staff
- [ ] Zero data loss incidents
- [ ] Zero cross-tenant data leakage

### Feedback Collection
- Weekly check-in call (30 min)
- In-app feedback button (sends to feedback@clinicflow.com)
- Structured survey at week 4 and week 8
- Slack channel for real-time support

### Incentives
- 3 months free after launch
- Input on product roadmap
- "Founding Clinic" badge on their portal

### Gradual Rollout (after beta)
1. Beta (5-10 clinics) → 8 weeks
2. Early access (20-50 clinics) → 4 weeks
3. General availability (unlimited) → launch
